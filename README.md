## What I do

Software architecture and AI engineering are my main focus. I design autonomous agent workflows with Model Context Protocol tooling, multi-agent coordination systems, and local LLM backends, backed by clean TypeScript and Python codebases.

When I step away from the IDE, I spend time flight simming with realistic airline procedures in simulators, sim racing, and building cosplay props through 3D printing, foam work, and detailed finishing.

My home lab runs on two bare-metal nodes and local workstations, acting as a living environment for my AI agents and daily infrastructure.

---

## Hardware and environment

- Custom Battle Station: AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, 64GB RAM, fast NVMe storage. Serves as my local LLM inference powerhouse, simulator rig, and workstation.
- Agir (Intel NUC): runs Home Assistant OS for smart home automation, lighting, and sensors across the space.
- Kashino (Unraid NAS): handles heavy storage arrays, Docker containers, and the persistent AI gateway ingress.
- Laffey (Bambu Lab H2C): high-speed 3D printer with multi-material capability for rapid prototyping and props.
