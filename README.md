## 🧭 What I do

**🤖 Software Architecture & AI Programming**
Senior Software Architect & AI Systems Engineer. I architect autonomous agent pipelines with
Model Context Protocol (MCP) tooling, multi-agent coordination and local LLM inference, combined
with strict type safety in TypeScript and React 19.

**✈️ Flight Simulation & Gaming**
Virtual aviator dedicated to realistic airline operational procedures — SimBrief dispatching,
BeyondATC radio, Airbus A320 / Boeing 737 / A350 and custom cockpit hardware. On wheels: sim
racing and open-world road trips with direct-drive telemetry.

**✨ Cosplay Art & Visual Craft**
The complete production cycle: 3D printing & foam fabrication, precision wig sculpting, custom
garment tailoring, studio & location shooting, colour grading in post-production.

**🛰️ Autonomous AI & Home Lab**
A living laboratory — two bare-metal nodes, smart-home automations and 3D additive manufacturing,
orchestrated by autonomous AI agents. The place where artificial intelligence, physical hardware
and daily living connect.
